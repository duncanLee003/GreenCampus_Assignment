Sustainability Tips

Small, practical changes that add up to a big difference. From cutting down on waste to greener ways of getting around — here's how you can start living more sustainably today.

Reduce waste by choosing reusables, composting scraps, and buying in bulk to cut down on packaging.

Save energy by switching off lights, unplugging idle devices, and making the most of natural daylight.

Travel greener by using public transport, carpooling, cycling, or walking whenever possible.

All Tips

Reducing Waste

Cut down on what goes to landfill
1

Reusable alternatives — switch to reusable water bottles, shopping bags, and food containers to cut single-use plastics.

2

Shop smartly — buy in bulk to reduce packaging waste, and choose products with minimal packaging where possible.

3

Compost food scraps and yard waste to reduce landfill waste and create nutrient-rich soil for your garden.

4

Repair before replacing — fix broken items where possible instead of throwing them away.

5

Buy second-hand — charity shops, swap events, and online marketplaces give items a longer life and reduce demand for new production.

Saving Energy

Lower your carbon footprint at home
1

Turn off lights and electronics when not in use — it adds up more than you'd think.

2

Use energy-efficient appliances with an A-rating when it's time to upgrade household items.

3

Unplug chargers and devices on standby — they still draw power even when not in use.

4

Use natural light during the day instead of artificial lighting wherever you can.

5

Set your thermostat to an energy-efficient temperature when you are away from home.

Greener Travel Options

Move around with fewer emissions
1

Use public transport or carpool when possible — buses and trains dramatically cut per-person emissions.

2

Walk or cycle for short journeys — zero emissions and great for your health too.

3

Combine errands into single trips to cut the number of journeys you make by car.
